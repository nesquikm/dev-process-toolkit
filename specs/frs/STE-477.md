# /implement § 4b′ propagation-commit subject must satisfy the 72-char commit-msg hook

## Summary

When an implementation deletes a tracked file, the toolkit makes a follow-up commit recording that the deletion was propagated into the cross-cutting specs. The subject line it prescribes embeds the deleted file's full path, and the toolkit's own commit hook rejects subjects longer than seventy-two characters. The fixed wording alone consumes fifty-five of those, so only very short paths fit at all. Two shipped surfaces of the same toolkit disagree, and the one that loses is the one a downstream project hits the first time it deletes a file.

## Requirement

The § 4b′ propagation-commit subject stops scaling with the removed path. The path moves to the commit body; the subject becomes a fixed-length form that fits inside 72 characters for every input. `--no-verify` remains rejected (deterministic gates override LLM judgment). A pre-write length assertion converts the residual bound into an enforced one. This is the STE-461 resolution shape, reapplied to a second site.

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-STE-477.1: `skills/implement/SKILL.md` § 4b′ prescribes a subject whose rendered length is independent of `<removed-path>`; the removed path appears in the commit body, not the subject.
- AC-STE-477.2: The prescribed subject renders ≤ 72 characters for every path in a fixture set including at least one path ≥ 60 characters; a unit test asserts the bound and fails when the template regresses to the path-embedding form.
- AC-STE-477.3: A pre-write length assertion runs before the propagation commit is issued and refuses with the NFR-10 canonical shape rather than emitting a subject the hook will reject; `--no-verify` appears on no path.
- AC-STE-477.4: `templates/git-hooks/commit-msg.sh` is byte-unchanged — the 72-char rule is the authority, not the thing that bends.
- AC-STE-477.5: `docs/implement-reference.md` § Phase 4b′ states the new subject shape and names the 72-char constraint as its reason.
- AC-STE-477.6: Notes record that `.claude/skills/smoke-test/SKILL.md` lines 998 and 1006 grep the retired literal and are repaired under STE-488 in M127 — the cross-milestone coupling is written down, not discovered later.

## Technical Design

Measured overhead: `chore(specs): propagate ` (24) + ` removal to cross-cutting specs` (31) = 55 fixed chars, leaving 17 for the path. `src/.placeholder.test.ts` renders 79; `plugins/dev-process-toolkit/adapters/_shared/src/foo.ts` renders 110. The template is unsatisfiable for nearly any realistic path, not merely long ones — which is why this reads as always-broken rather than edge-case.

STE-461 solved the same class at the LocalProvider lock-commit site: stop the subject scaling with the variable part, move the variable to the body, add a pre-write length assertion, and reject `--no-verify` explicitly. Reuse that shape rather than inventing a second one.

## Testing

Unit test over a path fixture set asserting the 72-char bound, with a mutation that restores the path-embedding form and must go red. Meta-test pin on the subject literal in the SKILL. `bun test` from `plugins/dev-process-toolkit`.
