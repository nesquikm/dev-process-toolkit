# Correct the tracker-less plan/FR id equality clause — it is unsatisfiable in general

## Summary

A Phase 4 verify row demands the milestone plan's minted identity be byte identical to the FR's. Two consecutive mints differ in their final character, and a milestone containing two FRs cannot be byte identical to both. The clause is unsatisfiable in general, not merely near missed on one run.

The shipped enforcing contract requires only that a plan derive its own filename from its own recorded identity, and that self derivation held on both artifacts of the live run. The criterion is wrong and the code is right.

This was never executed live before the 2026-08-08 conformance run, which is why it survived authoring, review and audit inside its own milestone.

## Requirement

Delete the equality clause from the acceptance criterion and from the Phase 4 verify row, keep the self derivation half verbatim in both, and re anchor the enforcing pin onto the surviving half rather than deleting it.
