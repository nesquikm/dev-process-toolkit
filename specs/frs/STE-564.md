# Fixture arms key on the subject, not on a rendering that moved

## Summary

A predecessor standardised how a probe row is rendered. Every fixture arm keyed to the old bold-row shape stopped matching that day, and none said so.

Measured across five 2026-09-05 captures: six arms occur zero times, while the probes they grade fired and reported file, line, expectation and actual. What failed is the assertion — the fourth occurrence of this class.

The diagnosis came from a contrast: a sibling keys on the probe name and passed.

## Requirement

Every fixture arm in groups 2 and 9 asserts its probe's own identity rather than a rendering of it, keeps the discrimination the row prefix was chosen to provide, and is proven able to fail — per group, against a genuinely unhealthy probe, rather than in aggregate. Group 2 stages a fixture its own leg can grade.

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-STE-564.1: `capability_row_assert.ts` gains TOKEN-BOUNDED scoring: the expectations `present-token`, `absent-token` and `any-of-token` match a key only where it is not adjacent to an identifier character (`[A-Za-z0-9_-]` on either side). `identity_mode_conditional` therefore does NOT match inside `plan_identity_mode_conditional`, while `plan_identity_mode_conditional` matches itself. Exported as a function and reachable from the CLI; the existing three expectations are byte-unchanged in behaviour, asserted rather than assumed.
- AC-STE-564.2: The token boundary is the ONLY thing that changed about scoring. Token-bounded scoring still decides on the assistant-text projection, still subtracts `postRefusalHits`, still reports `rawHits` as diagnostics that never decide, and still scores an absent or malformed capture as all-zero rather than throwing. Asserted against the same captures the plain expectations are asserted against.
- AC-STE-564.3: Group 2's three identifier arms key on probe #26's NAME under both rendered spellings — `any-of-token tracker-project-milestone-attached tracker_project_milestone_attached` — and carry no `**`, no `#26`, and no other row decoration. Group 2b's subject arm likewise scores probe #26's own failing row without a decoration in it.
- AC-STE-564.4: Group 9a's and 9b's identifier arms key on their probe names under both spellings, through `any-of-token`, and the substring discrimination 9a's prose demands is asserted directly: 9a's arm exits 0 on the tracker-block capture and NON-ZERO on the plan-stem capture, and 9b's does the reverse. Both directions, over the committed `m127-falsifiability` captures — this is the property the row prefix was protecting, and it is now protected by the matcher instead.
- AC-STE-564.5: Group 2 stages a fixture its own leg can grade. On a Linear leg the staged FR carries the tracker-mode frontmatter shape — a `tracker:` block, no `id:` — and its filename follows `Provider.filenameFor`, so probe #26's subject receives a well-formed input instead of a `mode: none`-shaped one. The group's prose records what the retired shape made vacuous, so the next reader does not restore it.
- AC-STE-564.6: Probe #26's `## Notes` token is READ. The group asserts non-vacuously that the staged capability key surfaced, which is the assertion the mis-shaped fixture was silently skipping.
- AC-STE-564.7: Every re-keyed arm is proven able to FAIL, PER GROUP and not in aggregate. For each of group 2 and group 9, a mutated capture with its probe's rendering stripped — the shape a genuinely non-firing probe would leave — reds that group's arm, while the unmutated capture leaves it green. Aggregate falsifiability is what let four arms die together without a single red.
- AC-STE-564.8: No arm anywhere in the harness SKILLs keys a probe identifier on a row decoration. Enforced by a DERIVED SWEEP over the fixture-group spans, not by an enumerated list of the six known arms: an enumerated list is exactly the shape that let these six survive a standardisation aimed at the surface they read. A violation names the file, the line, and the decoration it carries.
- AC-STE-564.9: Falsifiability — AC.1, AC.4, AC.7 and AC.8 are mutation-tested. Disabling the boundary check reds AC.1 by re-admitting the substring false green; swapping either group-9 arm for its sibling's reds AC.4; leaving a group's arm decoration-keyed reds AC.7 for that group and no other; adding a decoration-keyed arm to a fixture span reds AC.8. Each mutation reds ITS OWN leg with its siblings green.

## Technical Design

Token-bounded matching is added beside the existing scan rather than replacing it: the boundary is a predicate over the two characters adjacent to a hit, so a caller wanting the current behaviour keeps it unchanged.

The arms live in a repo-root project-local skill, outside the plugin tree. A sweep scoped to the plugin root cannot see it, so the sweep is rooted at the repo and reads the same skill-path constant the artifact scan already uses.

The sweep grades arms, not prose. Both groups' paragraphs quote the retired shapes while explaining them, and a check that could not tell an argument from an assertion would forbid writing the explanation down.

## Testing

The re-keyed arms are scored against the committed `m127-falsifiability` captures, which are inputs rather than run outputs, so the assertions run under `bun test` with no smoke run.

Falsifiability is per group: each group's arm is scored against a mutated copy of its own capture with the probe's rendering removed, and the mutation is routed through the throwing `mutate` helper so a pattern that stopped matching is a loud error rather than a green test.

## Notes

The five 2026-09-05 captures measuring the zero occurrences are run outputs and are not committed. The committed 2026-08-16 captures carry the OLD rendering, making them the right input for the substring-discrimination legs and the wrong input for a claim about today's runtime. This FR asserts the arms are subject-keyed — true of both renderings.
