# One documented fixture-roster spelling across every leg

## Summary

Two of the three runs report their fixture roster one line per group, in the spelling the harness documents. The third compacts the whole roster onto one line. Both are readable by a person, but anything searching for the documented spelling finds nothing at all on the third — which is the shape of a silent skip, indistinguishable from a run that reported nothing.

## Requirement

Every leg emits the documented one-line-per-group roster spelling. The compact form is not an accepted alternative, and the rule is stated once rather than in two places that can drift.

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-STE-491.1: The harness states the roster spelling once, as the single accepted form, at the point a leg reads before rendering its findings file.
- AC-STE-491.2: A search for the documented `runtime check:` spelling returns one match per rostered group on every leg, including the leg that previously compacted.
- AC-STE-491.3: Fixture group 12's own contract, which names the spelling independently, is reconciled with the general rule — one statement, not two.
- AC-STE-491.4: Notes record that no automated consumer depended on this at the time of the fix, so the change is preventive rather than corrective.

## Technical Design

Measured: linear and none emit one `STE-<N> runtime check: PASS|FAIL|N/A` line per group; jira emits a compact `STE-226 PASS · STE-214 N/A · …` form. Both were parseable by hand during aggregation, which is why this scored low — but a consumer grepping the documented spelling finds zero matches on the jira leg, and zero matches is exactly what a leg that skipped the roster entirely would produce.

**Corrected during implementation — the original premise here was wrong twice, and both corrections are load-bearing.** This section first read: "the spelling is stated in the smoke-test SKILL generally and again inside group 12's own contract. Two statements of one rule…". Measured on the shipped file, `grep -c 'runtime check:'` returns **fourteen**, not two: the general statement plus **all thirteen** fixture-group footers, each spelling `STE-<sut> runtime check: <LABEL>` inline, seven of which additionally restate the general rule's NOT-REACHED / N/A semantics. Group 12 was singled out because two shipped suites read its PASS/FAIL literals (`m124-ste-467`, `m117-ste-425`), not because it was the only duplicate.

The causal claim was also wrong. Two-statement drift did **not** cause the compaction: the authority module `adapters/_shared/src/smoke_fixture_groups.ts` already emitted one canonical line per rostered group on every leg, jira included, before this FR. The compact form is what **hand-tallying** produces when a leg does not run the module at all. So the structural repair is to state, at the point a leg reads before writing its findings file, that the module is the producer and the compact form is refused — not merely to deduplicate a rule the module was already obeying.

## Testing

Roster-spelling check asserting one match per rostered group **against the authority module's real per-leg output** for all three legs, with the group count derived from the roster rather than restated; a compact `·`-joined rendering of the same roster must fail the same check. Prose pins on § Phase 3 — Capture (the rule stated once, at the render point) and on group 12's citation. `bun test` full root.

**Not testable as originally written, and named rather than absorbed.** This section first specified the check "against archived findings files from all three legs". No findings file is ever committed — they live at `/tmp/dpt-smoke-findings-<date>-<tracker>.md` and are LLM-rendered per run — so that test cannot exist in this repo. The renderer is the producer of those lines and is the satisfiable subject; the shipped test says so in its own header.

## Notes

Origin: 2026-08-16 /conformance-loop iter-1, driver-level finding D2 (low). Noted rather than escalated at capture time because nothing automated was reading the roster then.

The subject of that claim is the documented `STE-<N> runtime check: <LABEL>` roster spelling — naming it is what makes "nothing read it" checkable at all. No automated consumer depended on that spelling at capture time, and none depended on it at the time of the fix either: the compacted leg reddened no gate, no probe and no test, and aggregation was done by hand.

The change is therefore preventive rather than corrective. It closes a silent-skip shape before a consumer starts depending on the spelling; it repairs no broken reader, because at the time of the fix there was no reader to break. Recorded here so that framing survives into the changelog rather than reading as a bug fix for a failure that never happened.

## Implementation notes

- **AC.3 is satisfied by definition, not by reconciliation.** "One statement, not two" undercounts the duplication by eleven: `grep -c 'runtime check:'` on the shipped SKILL returns fourteen — the general statement plus all thirteen fixture-group footers — and seven footers additionally restate the general rule's NOT-REACHED / N/A semantics. One of thirteen was reconciled (group 12, singled out only because two shipped suites read its literals); twelve still spell the token inline and six of the seven semantic restatements survive. The AC passes under its own three-conjunct definition of "statement", under which those twelve were never statements. Follow-up recorded.
- **AC.2 is preservation-only, measured rather than assumed.** Restoring the pre-change SKILL and FR in a scratch mirror gives 17 pass / 12 fail with **zero** reds in AC.2 — the renderer already emitted one canonical line per rostered group on every leg, jira included, and `smoke_fixture_groups.ts` is untouched by this milestone. The four discriminators falsify the check function, not the delivery. AC.2's stated subject is a *leg*, and the compaction was never a renderer output but what hand-tallying produces when a leg does not run the module; no in-repo assertion can reach that subject, since findings files are never committed. The shipped work that addresses it is § Phase 3's "produced, not typed" prose — AC.1's deliverable. The audit classified it Partial and it was not upgraded.
- **AC.1's "stated once" predicate is a three-literal co-occurrence test, not a normativity test**, and it is one token wide in both directions. Six sections sit one token from flipping it: sub-fixtures 2c, 9c, 10c, 11a and 13a satisfy two of three conjuncts (token + labels, no module basename), and § Phase 2.X summary line satisfies the other two since this FR removed the token from its bullet. Verified by mutation — prepending a bare "Rendered by `smoke_fixture_groups.ts`." to group 13a's footer FALSE-REDs the stated-once assertion without that footer restating anything. So the out-of-scope footer rewrite cannot name the module in a footer without first revisiting the predicate.
- The `smoke_fixture_groups.ts render --leg …` invocation now appears twice — § Phase 2.X's fence (pre-dating this FR) and § Phase 3's new paragraph. Only the Phase 3 copy is pinned, so the Phase 2.X copy can drift silently. Deduplicating it means deleting a runnable command from the runbook step where the operator actually runs it, which is a behaviour change to a section no AC covers. Left deliberately; recorded as follow-up.
