# STE-423: Tracker-scope every smoke artifact path and pidfile glob {#STE-423}

## Summary

The smoke skill promises that running both trackers at once is safe by construction, because every per-run artifact is keyed on the tracker and the two runs never touch the same path. Two surfaces break that promise, and both were live during this run.

The first writes its transcript fixtures to a path keyed only on the date, and the skill reasons explicitly that including the date is what prevents concurrent collision. A date cannot disambiguate two same-day legs, and two same-day legs is precisely the sanctioned mode.

The second is worse. The pre-final-message liveness fence walks an unscoped pattern that matches both legs, and its abort branch instructs the driver to terminate every process it finds and then delete the matching files. The identity check does not protect the victim, because the other leg's child is a genuine process of the expected kind and passes the guard.

One surface silently clobbers evidence; the other destroys a running partner's work. Both legs noticed and deviated from the documented behavior to avoid harming each other, which is why the run survived.

## Requirement

Every per-run smoke artifact path and every pidfile glob used for liveness detection or reaping MUST carry the `<tracker>` segment, so that no leg can read, overwrite, signal, or delete another leg's state.

Verified 2026-07-27 by reading the skill:

- Phase 8 fixture path is `tests/fixtures/socratic-first-turn/<skill>-<YYYY-MM-DD>.json` (`SKILL.md:1165`), with the adjacent rationale asserting *"The fixture filename includes the date so concurrent runs don't collide"* — the claim is the defect.
- The unscoped glob `/tmp/dpt-smoke-*.pid` appears at `SKILL.md:339`, `:383`, `:445` and `:453` — all three abort clauses plus the canonical fenced snippet.

Live exposure, observed rather than inferred: while the Jira leg was in Phase 8 the Linear leg held `/tmp/dpt-smoke-linear-phase8-spec-write.pid` with an answering PID and had just written two of the four identical fixture filenames. Had the Jira leg taken its abort branch at that moment, the documented fence would have terminated the Linear leg's grandchildren and deleted its pidfiles.

Both deviations are recorded in the run: the Jira leg wrote `<skill>-jira-<DATE>.json` and scoped its own reap to `/tmp/dpt-smoke-jira-*.pid`.

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-STE-423.1: The Phase 8 fixture path carries the tracker segment, and the accompanying rationale no longer claims that a date-keyed filename prevents concurrent collision.
- AC-STE-423.2: Every pidfile glob used for liveness detection or reaping in the smoke SKILL carries the tracker segment — all four occurrences, not only the fenced snippet.
- AC-STE-423.3: A tandem fixture proves non-vacuity: a simulated abort on one leg leaves a concurrently-live partner leg's pidfiles and processes untouched.
- AC-STE-423.4: A probe asserts that no smoke artifact path or pidfile glob in the skill lacks the tracker segment, so a future edit cannot reintroduce an unscoped one.
- AC-STE-423.5: The parallelism-safety claim in the operator-driven-parallelism section is re-derived from the corrected paths and states the invariant it can actually support.

## Technical Design

Mechanically small — add the segment to the fixture path and to four globs — but the parity trap is real: a fix that reaches only the fenced snippet and misses the three prose clauses leaves the hazard live in exactly the branches that perform the destructive action. AC-STE-423.2 is worded to require all four.

AC-STE-423.4's probe is the durable part. A grep-shape meta-test over the skill asserting that every `dpt-smoke-` path literal carries `linear` or `jira` (or the `<tracker>` placeholder) turns this from a one-time correction into an enforced invariant.

Note the abort clauses also omit a null-glob guard in their prose form, so under the operator's shell an unmatched glob raises an error rather than expanding to nothing. Worth correcting in the same pass.

## Testing

- Meta-test per AC-STE-423.4 over the smoke SKILL.
- A tandem simulation per AC-STE-423.3 with two pidfile sets, asserting a scoped reap touches only its own.
- Byte assertions that all four glob sites carry the segment.

## Notes

Merges two findings from the run that share one invariant and one fix shape — the artifact-clobber surface and the cross-leg-kill surface. They were filed separately by the Jira leg; keeping them in one FR avoids a partial fix that closes the cosmetic half and leaves the destructive half open.

The shell-portability detail is not incidental: the driver runs under a shell where an unmatched glob is an error, and that behavior bit this run's own tooling twice.
