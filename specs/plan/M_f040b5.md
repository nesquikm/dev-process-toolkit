### Why F4 is first

`push_ac_toggle` was withheld on a green gate with a Done ticket, and four acceptance criteria never toggled. The branch read `feat/m_b11423-greet-helper`: the default template is `{type}/m{N}-{slug}`, and under tracker-first minting `{N}` renders `M_<6-hex>`, so Tier-1 branch-regex resolution finds no ticket id and Tier-2 needs a human. The template and the resolver disagree by construction on every Linear project using the defaults, which makes an already-shipped feature unreachable rather than merely awkward.

### What made F1 and F2 findable

Neither leg could see it. Different groups, different probes, different legs — only aggregation across both put them side by side, and the none leg records it as the fourth occurrence of the class. The diagnosis came from a contrast rather than from a failure: sub-fixture 3c keys on the probe **name** instead of a bold marker and passed on the same runtime that failed the others. An assertion pinned to how something renders dies the day the rendering is standardised, and says nothing about whether the subject is healthy.
