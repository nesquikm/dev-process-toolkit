# Implementation Plan

## M_685ff6 — Honest Join, Closed Gate {#M_685ff6}

**Goal:** Every decision that binds one repository's work to a milestone container, or releases a milestone two repositories share, is taken by executed code. The decision reports what it will do before it does it, and it refuses when it cannot prove its answer. A join says it is a join and names the sibling. The span is declared on both sides. The release gate reads the sibling's whole repository and holds whenever the sibling is not provably idle. A repoint is checked row by row and rewrites one line.

**Prerequisites:** Ships SECOND, after M_947c79, and before M_85e846 and M_2306b6. This milestone reads `repo_tag`, `min_dpt_version` and the `shared` flag through STE-602, writes receipts through the same FR's store, calls the sub-section writer and extends probe #25 after STE-603, and turns the container-call Reminder of STE-607 into rules. Milestones ship serially: parallel milestones collide on the probe-count pins, the reachability ledger and the release files.

**Release target:** v2.88.0, a minor bump over M_947c79's v2.87.0, because two FRs are `Added` (the span writer and the repoint command) and the rest are `Fixed`; if another release lands between, the next minor after it. Verify with `inferBump` against the FR frontmatter and CHANGELOG rather than trusting this line. After release, shared repositories raise `min_dpt_version` to this version. A client between M_947c79's floor and this one still joins silently.

### Why this order

The join decision comes first: the span writer extends its front door, and the pre-create hook rule sits beside its container-write rules. The gate's identity and git-state reader come before the span writer, which resolves paths against the same main worktree root and adds its `one-sided` state to the same set. The pre-create check precedes the repoint, because the repoint's old-container rule assumes a stranded plan can no longer mint an orphan ticket. The repoint is last: its probe #25 leg reuses the attach refusal's forward sanitization, and it calls M_947c79's writer.
