### Recorded runs

- **Frame drift, unfixed renderer (2026-10-13, ffmpeg 9.0.2, AC-STE-674.3).** Fixture: a variable-frame-rate clip at 30 fps to 1.0 s, no frame until 2.5 s, then 10 fps. The EDL places it first (`in` 0, `out` 2), then two 1 s clips. Measured first frames: clip 1 at 0.000 s (placed 0), clip 2 at 2.500 s (placed 2.0, +0.5 s), clip 3 at 3.500 s (placed 3.0, +0.5 s). The 4.000 s duration matched the EDL end only because the output cap hid the overrun. Cause: `trim` keeps the last in-window frame (t = 1.0) with its 1.5 s source duration, and the later `fps=30` fills frames up to 2.5 s. The segment runs 2.5 s instead of 2.0 s, so every later clip shifts by the gap. Over a long recording these gaps add up, as in the first video's 9 s drift.
