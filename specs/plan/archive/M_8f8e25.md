# Implementation Plan

## M_8f8e25 — Pins that punish the right direction {#M_8f8e25}

**Goal:** A guard does not fail when the thing it guards gets better.

**Prerequisites:** None. Ships after M_dc2ecb.

**Release target:** the next release after v2.80.1 — verify against `CHANGELOG.md` rather than trusting this line.

Probe #81 counts ordered-and-unreachable references and pins the number. Reachability is transitive over the import graph, so giving any module a front door — the behaviour the probe exists to encourage — lowers the count and reds the gate. M140 met this head-on: its FR mandated both a shared parser and an entry point while separately asserting the pin had not moved, which no design satisfies.

Lowering is the sanctioned direction and the pin has never been raised across four lowerings. The problem is not the rule, it is the cost of obeying it.

### Why the two FRs ride together

M143 declined to register its scanners, and its reasoning was sound: a registration costs a line probe #81 counts and ripples across many pinned counts, so it was an FR rather than a quiet addition. That is precisely the coupling STE-557 removes. Once lowering costs one edit, registering a scanner stops being a milestone-sized decision — so STE-558 is both the second half of the fix and its first real exercise.
