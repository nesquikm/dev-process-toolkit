# Implementation Plan

## M120 — Jira Milestone-Identity Enforcement {#M120}

**Goal:** Close the `mode: jira` half of the milestone-identity gap that M119 closed for `mode: none`. Probe #73's tracker arm checks only that no plan carries an `id:` line, so a sequential `M<N>` plan in a Jira project is fully gate-clean — which is why two consumer projects accumulated five of them in the fortnight after Epic-first shipped, with a clean gate every run. `/spec-write`'s own prose compounds it by instructing two different behaviours depending on which section a reader enters, and `/gate-check` documents a wiring that does not exist.

**Prerequisites:** None. STE-443 and STE-444 are independent and may land in either order.

**Release target:** v2.60.0

**Migration:** `none` — no consumer-artifact convention changes. The epoch is set to this release's ship date precisely so every plan extant in every consumer project is grandfathered; nothing needs rewriting anywhere, which is why there is no registry entry to bind.

**Provenance:** field investigation 2026-08-04 against two Jira consumer projects (keys GF and GB), verified against live Jira (zero Epics exist; FR Tasks unparented, carrying `milestone-M<N>` labels) and against this repository at v2.59.0. Design session: `/dev-process-toolkit:brainstorm`, same date. Four independent traces plus two adversarial verifications confirmed the next milestone in those projects would again be sequential.
