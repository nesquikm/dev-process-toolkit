### Why the README omission matters more than it looks

The sentence above the diagram reads "the toolkit groups its 18 user-invoked skills into a four-phase lifecycle — read left-to-right for the full path". The diagram has fifteen nodes. That framing is what converts an omission into a misdirection: it tells the reader they have seen everything, before they reach the prose that says otherwise. The prose then accounts for two of the three absentees, and the doc it points at for "the mechanics it omits" does not mention the third at all.
